The gap

The model says what should be paid. Nothing proves what is being paid.

Plan

Contract rates, budgets, forecasts, cost drivers. Modelled and reviewed.

Invoice

Arrives from the contractor with hours, rates and a reference. Usually a PDF and a spreadsheet.

Proof

Who attested the hours? Which rate version applied? Was this line already claimed?

Most mining cost-control failures are not modelling failures. They are evidence failures at the payment boundary.

What actually goes wrong

Four recurring failure modes.

  • Rate drift. The invoice uses a rate that was superseded mid-period. The spreadsheet uses the old one.
  • Unattested hours. Operations never confirmed the hours, but the invoice still gets processed.
  • Double claiming. The same shift reference is billed again on the next invoice.
  • Approval theatre. The person who prepared the invoice also approves it. Nobody can prove otherwise later.
MineAssure3
Ask: "Which of these four have you seen at a client?" Let them talk. Their answer tells you which pilot to propose.

What MineAssure does

One controlled path from contract to approved cost.

01 / TERMS

Freeze the contract

Approved rate, work code, effective dates and cap. Versioned; approved by a distinct role.

02 / EVIDENCE

Attest the work

Hours are independently verified. The importer cannot attest their own record.

03 / ASSESS

Reconcile the invoice

Deterministic rules classify every line. Inputs are frozen and hashed.

04 / DECIDE

Approve and export

Finance approves an amount. The export carries run, approval and input hashes.

No black box. Same inputs, same result, every time — and the decision is a human one.
MineAssure4
Language: "Deterministic" matters to a modeller. Contrast with AI invoice extraction. The system never decides to pay; it decides what is supportable.

Why it holds up

Designed for the audit, not the demo.

Frozen inputs

The assessed invoice, rate version and evidence are snapshotted and hashed. A later change cannot rewrite history.

Separation of duties

Preparer, verifier, approver and auditor are distinct grants. Self-approval and stale approvals are rejected.

Immutable decisions

Approvals are versioned and signed. Partial approval is a first-class outcome, not a workaround.

Reproducible export

Approved lines only, integer cents, provenance and artifact hash. Repeat the request; get the same artefact.
