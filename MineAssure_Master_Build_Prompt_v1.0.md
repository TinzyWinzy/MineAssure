## Non-negotiable product behavior
MineAssure verifies mining contractor invoices against approved contract terms and independently attested operational work evidence, identifies explainable exceptions, routes disputed/unverified lines to authorized humans, and exports **only approved** invoice actuals. This is not an automated payment system, fraud determiner or ERP replacement. Keep clear categories for `invoiced`, `quantified_variance`, `unverified_amount`, `provisionally_supported`, `approved_amount`, `confirmed_adjustment`, and `recovered_cash` (only with separately evidenced recovery). Never call an alert savings. Missing/ambiguous evidence yields **undetermined or unverified**, NOT a confident zero-payable or proven overcharge. Avoid financial double-counting across rules and across invoices. Every displayed amount must be drillable to line, data, formula, policy and review status.

## Functional P0 vertical slice
1. Admin creates organisation/site and assigns scoped users/roles.
2. Contract preparer drafts equipment-hire contract with service window, hourly work code, USD hourly rate, unit, caps, minimum/standby explicit policy flags and supporting original document; distinct contract approver approves effective-dated version.
3. Operations verifier imports independently sourced shift/equipment logs via CSV/XLSX and optionally manually registers work, preserves original file and attests verified quantities; rejected rows visible with precise reasons. Incomplete evidence is unverified.
4. AP analyst imports invoice CSV/XLSX and lines using schema-mapping wizard; store original file/checksum, staging/rejected rows, supplier invoice number and line fingerprints. Duplicate reference and fuzzy/near duplicate across supplier/site/period flagged for human determination, not arbitrarily discarded.
5. Reconciliation selects **approved** rate version effective on service date (including only authorized variations), normalizes units, links evidence, calculates invoice and supported components using Decimal, checks R001 rate, R002 quantity, R003 duplicates, R004 contract dates, R005 work conflicts, R006 variations, R007 document gaps, R008 caps; produces line-level explanations, eligible vs unverified balances, evidence allocation and unique non-double-counted quantified exposure. Use deterministic frozen snapshots and rule version; repeatable results for same snapshot. New evidence or contract approval creates a new immutable run, never silently alters an old one.
6. Evidence allocation must prevent a single shift/equipment record from being consumed twice across different invoices, including concurrent approval/reconciliation; use transactions, row locking/serializable safeguards and tests for contention. Design reservation vs final consumption semantics; release/revoke allocations on supersession or reversal with audit trail.
7. Exception reviewer assigns and investigates findings, requests additional evidence, records comments and dispute proposals. Finance approver can fully/partially approve only fully determined eligible amounts within authority, with explicit materiality/SoD checks. Approval of disputed amounts requires documented override policy, distinct authorization, and audit. Approval uses version/ETag or optimistic concurrency and rejects stale run/version.
8. Export only reviewer-approved cost lines, identified by unique export batch, with tenant/site/contractor/work code/period/currency/approved minor-unit amount and provenance. Never include unresolved/unsupported amounts. Re-run/re-download are idempotent with immutable artifact/hash; export batch totals equal approved lines and audit is inspectable.
9. Dashboard: invoice totals, provisionally supported, unverified, quantified variance, approved, open disputes, age/status; all labeled clearly, filtered by tenant/site/date and drill through to line-level sources; totals must be mathematically reconcilable and avoid counting multiple reconciliation runs.

## Deterministic golden scenarios and exact expectations
A. Approved USD 240/hour contract, 160 hours invoiced at $250/hour, 145 independently verified eligible hours => invoice $40,000.00, provisional supported $34,800.00 and **quantifiable discrepancy $5,200.00** if no variation/standby/minimum/tax applies; findings R001 and R002. Both findings must NOT each add their own inflated exposure.
B. No evidence for 10 hours invoiced at $240: invoice $2,400.00, `unverified_amount = $2,400.00`, `quantified_variance = undetermined/0 only if explicitly distinguished` and **no approved payable**. Do not label $2,400 confirmed overcharge.
C. Same evidence work reference billed by another invoice: new invoice is flagged with an allocation conflict; cannot support twice, even if invoice references differ. Concurrency test must confirm one unit of verified evidence is not allocated twice.
D. Rate revision from $240 to $255 effective 15 October: earlier service dates use $240, later ones $255; historical previously reconciled runs remain reproducible.
E. Draft or unapproved contract variation does not authorize extra payment.
F. Wrong-tenant or unassigned-site read/write, IDOR access, self-approval, stale-review approval: fail safely with proper HTTP status and audit.
G. Partially approved invoice exports only approved lines; totals and export hash match persisted approved decision snapshots; repeated export request cannot duplicate postings.
H. Duplicate invoice references and same-shift duplicate lines flag separately. Missing evidence, ambiguous evidence, and out-of-contract periods have distinct states.
